## FFmpeg-derived files

These files are derived from the FFmpeg sources listed above:

- `crates/formats/src/smacker/mod.rs` (container and audio flag handling)
- `crates/formats/src/smacker/huff.rs` (Huffman trees)
- `crates/formats/src/smacker/video.rs` (palette and video blocks)
- `crates/formats/src/smacker/audio.rs` (Smacker DPCM audio)

## Upscaler runner and models

The upscaled art pack is generated locally by the user from their own game data. The Real-ESRGAN runner and model weights are not in this repository (gitignored `tools/upscalers/`, fetched by `tools/fetch-upscaler.sh` with pinned SHA-256 values in `tools/upscalers/upscaler.lock.json`). They are bundled only in the release packages, which ship the licence texts from `tools/upscalers/licenses/` (sources and hashes in the lock file) and the vcomp140.dll terms. The generated pack contains derived art from the user's own game data and is never distributed.
