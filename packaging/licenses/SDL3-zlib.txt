SDL 3.4.20 licence texts, shipped with Milionar
================================================

Milionar links SDL 3.4.20 statically. The SDL source comes from the sdl3-src
3.4.20 crate on crates.io (checksum in Cargo.lock), built with CMake by sdl3-sys.
The texts below are copied unchanged from that crate:

  SDL/LICENSE.txt                    sha256 1c040b8271b37e5076359f8fd54240e371114112924d2df81ef87c7d6a1dfdfd
  SDL/src/hidapi/LICENSE.txt         sha256 7d3b087c34f35d4d538e3bcddd1ff8f66e92f9ef336881999482800ddf840913
  SDL/src/hidapi/LICENSE-bsd.txt     sha256 30eb1bef29b46f8ba7ab8b416035dbd93cb034a45481dd97815b944284582cd2
  SDL/src/video/yuv2rgb/LICENSE      sha256 f7d74004cc75778cae46c5f0139f244d67c07ef64cf51937e8272352197dcb18

HIDAPI is part of SDL's joystick support and may be used under one of three
licences; Milionar uses it under the BSD-style licence below.

SDL's own math library (SDL/src/libm, from FreeBSD/SunPro) carries the Sun
Microsystems notice of its source headers, reproduced at the end (text as in
SDL/src/libm/e_atan2.c and the other files of that folder).
